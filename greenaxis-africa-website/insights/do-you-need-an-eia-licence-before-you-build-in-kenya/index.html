Do You Need an EIA Licence Before You Build in Kenya?

If you are planning to build, expand or start a business activity in Kenya, one of the first questions is whether you need an environmental impact assessment. Here is a practical overview.

The short answer

Under the Environmental Management and Co-ordination Act, projects in the categories listed in the law must undergo an environmental impact assessment and receive a NEMA licence before they begin. Many residential, commercial and industrial developments fall in these categories.

Project report or study report?

Kenya applies a risk-based approach. Lower-risk projects typically need a project report, while larger or higher-risk projects need a full study report with more detailed baseline work and consultation. Screening your project is the way to know which applies.

What happens if you skip it?

Starting work without the required approvals can lead to enforcement action, stop-work orders and delays in financing, sale or occupation. Fixing the position later is usually more expensive than doing it properly at the start.

What to do first

Write a short description of your project: what it is, how big it is and where it is located. Share it with a consultant who can screen it and tell you which reports and approvals apply. Also check what county approvals you will need so both tracks run together.

This article is general information, not legal advice. Requirements vary and change, so confirm what applies to your project.
