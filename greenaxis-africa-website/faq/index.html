Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers on approvals, licences and compliance in Kenya.

General questions

What is an EIA and when is it required in Kenya?

An Environmental Impact Assessment studies the likely effects of a project on the environment and community. Projects in the categories listed under the law need NEMA approval before they start. We screen your project and tell you whether a project report or a full study report applies.

What is the difference between an EIA and an environmental audit?

An EIA is done before a project starts to predict impacts. An environmental audit is done on an operating project to check compliance and performance, usually on a regular basis.

What is the difference between change of user and extension of use?

A change of user moves a property from one approved use to another, for example residential to commercial. An extension of use expands or adds to what is already permitted on the plot.

My building was constructed without approvals. Can you help?

Often yes. Development regularization brings an existing development into compliance with county and environmental requirements. We assess your case first and tell you honestly what is possible.

How long do approvals take?

Timelines depend on the project type, how complete your documents are and the review process of the relevant authority. After reviewing your project we give you a realistic timeline.

How do I get started?

Call 0718 991 272, send us a WhatsApp message or fill in the quote form. Tell us what you are planning and where, and we will advise on the approvals you need.

Environmental Impact Assessment (EIA) Studies

Do I need an EIA before I start building?

If your project falls within the categories listed in the law, you generally need NEMA approval before work begins. We screen your project first and tell you which report applies.

What is the difference between a project report and a study report?

A project report is typically used for lower-risk projects, while a full study report is required for higher-risk or larger projects. The right route depends on the project type, size and location.

Environmental Audits (EA)

How often is an environmental audit required?

Operating projects are generally required to submit audit reports regularly, often annually. We confirm the schedule that applies to your facility.

What is an environmental control audit?

It is an audit for an existing facility that began operating without going through an EIA, used to assess its impacts and bring it into compliance.

NEMA Licensing & Permits

How do I know which NEMA licence I need?

It depends on what you are building or operating. Share a short description of your project and we will tell you which licences and reports apply.

Can you handle licence renewals?

Yes. We can track your renewal dates and prepare the audits and documents needed to renew on time.

Change of User Applications

Is a change of user the same as an extension of use?

No. A change of user moves a property from one approved use to another, while an extension of use expands or adds to the permitted use. See our Extension of Use page for details.

Can the application be refused?

It can be, for example if the proposed use conflicts with zoning or local plans. We assess feasibility upfront so you know the risks before you invest.

Extension of Use Approvals

What documents are usually needed?

Typically a title or lease, existing approvals, plans and supporting forms. Requirements vary by county, so we confirm the checklist for your plot.

Will an extension of use require an EIA?

Sometimes. If the expanded activity falls within the categories that require environmental assessment, NEMA approval may also be needed. We check this early.

Development Regularization

Can any unapproved building be regularised?

Not always. Buildings that breach zoning or safety standards may not qualify. We assess your case honestly before you spend on applications.

Will I need an environmental audit?

For some existing developments, yes. An environmental control audit may be part of regularising your position with NEMA.

Renovation Permits

Do small renovations need a permit?

Cosmetic work usually does not, but structural changes, extensions and changes to external appearance generally do. Tell us what you plan and we will confirm.

Who prepares the drawings?

Drawings are normally prepared by the registered professionals the county requires. We coordinate so the application is complete.

Air Quality Tests

Which pollutants do you test?

This depends on your activity and requirements, but commonly includes particulate matter and selected gases. We agree the parameters with you before testing.

Can the report be used for my EA or licence?

Yes. Air quality results are commonly included in audits and licence applications.

Noise Surveys

When should I do a noise survey?

Before starting noisy works, when preparing an audit or licence application, or after a complaint. Early surveys help you fix issues before they become disputes.

Can noise surveys be combined with other tests?

Yes. We often combine noise and air quality monitoring with environmental audits to save you time.

NEMA Licences for Plastic Packaging

Does my business need a plastic packaging licence?

It depends on whether you produce, import or use plastic packaging and in what quantities. Send us a short description and we will confirm what applies.

Do the rules change often?

They have been evolving, which is why we confirm current requirements for you before preparing any application.

County Government Approvals & Compliance Support

Why do county applications get delayed?

Most delays come from incomplete documents or missing approvals from other bodies. A complete pack submitted in the right order helps avoid them.

Can you handle approvals and NEMA licensing together?

Yes. Coordinating both from one team reduces back-and-forth and keeps your timeline realistic.

Still have a question?

Call or message us and tell us what you are planning. We will tell you which approvals you need.
